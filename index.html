About

Researching how policy works in practice.

I am a master's student in the International Economic Development Program at Hiroshima University. My research combines quantitative policy evaluation with close attention to institutions, implementation, and local context.

I am currently preparing for doctoral study and developing a broader research agenda on agricultural transformation, regional inequality, and the heterogeneous effects of public policy.
