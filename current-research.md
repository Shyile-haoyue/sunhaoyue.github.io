      <p class="article-lead">My research examines how public policy shapes agricultural development and regional economic change.</p>

      <h2>Research Interests</h2>
      <p>I am interested in policy evaluation, applied microeconomics, agricultural economics, regional development, and causal inference. Across these areas, I pay particular attention to how institutions and local implementation influence policy outcomes.</p>

      <h2>Ongoing Work</h2>
      <p>My current graduate research studies a place-based agricultural development policy in China. Using regional panel data and quasi-experimental methods, I examine whether the policy changes local agricultural outcomes and whether its effects differ across places and over time.</p>

      <h2>Research Direction</h2>
      <p>Looking ahead, I hope to develop research on agricultural transformation, regional inequality, and heterogeneous policy effects. I am especially interested in connecting rigorous empirical analysis with the institutional settings in which policies are implemented.</p>

      <p class="notice">Working papers, the master's thesis, and detailed research proposals are not posted publicly. Relevant materials are available upon request.</p>
