Research Overview

Current Research

My research examines how public policy shapes agricultural development and regional economic change.

Research Interests

I am interested in policy evaluation, applied microeconomics, agricultural economics, regional development, and causal inference. Across these areas, I pay particular attention to how institutions and local implementation influence policy outcomes.

Ongoing Work

My current graduate research studies a place-based agricultural development policy in China. Using regional panel data and quasi-experimental methods, I examine whether the policy changes local agricultural outcomes and whether its effects differ across places and over time.

Research Direction

Looking ahead, I hope to develop research on agricultural transformation, regional inequality, and heterogeneous policy effects. I am especially interested in connecting rigorous empirical analysis with the institutional settings in which policies are implemented.

Working papers, the master's thesis, and detailed research proposals are not posted publicly. Relevant materials are available upon request.
